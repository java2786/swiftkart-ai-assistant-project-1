SWIFTKART SHIPPING & DELIVERY POLICY

At SwiftKart, we ensure fast, reliable, and affordable shipping to customers across the country.

STANDARD SHIPPING:
- Delivery Timeline: 3-5 business days
- Service Area: Pan-India (including remote areas)
- Shipping Cost: FREE for orders ≥ ₹500 | ₹49 for orders < ₹500
- Tracking: Real-time updates via SMS and email
- Carrier: SwiftExpress (primary), BlueDart (secondary)

EXPRESS SHIPPING:
- Delivery Timeline: 1-2 business days (available in major metros)
- Available Cities: Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai
- Shipping Cost: ₹99 (FREE for orders ≥ ₹2000)
- Guaranteed delivery or money-back guarantee

SAME-DAY DELIVERY:
- Available: 8 AM - 8 PM in select ZIP codes
- Shipping Cost: ₹149
- Must order before 12 PM for same-day delivery
- Not available on holidays

SHIPPING PARTNERS:
1. SwiftExpress - Our primary logistics partner
   - Service Coverage: 100% India
   - Reliability Rating: 99.5%
   - Average Delivery: 3-5 days (standard)

2. BlueDart - Secondary partner for remote areas
   - Service Coverage: All India with extended reach
   - Average Delivery: 4-6 days (standard)
   - Express: 1-2 days (metro areas only)

DELIVERY DETAILS:
- Packages are delivered between 10 AM - 6 PM on weekdays
- Weekend and evening delivery available in metro areas (+₹50)
- Delivery attempts are made twice before package is returned
- Customer can reschedule delivery up to 2 times via tracking page

INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING:
- Currently NOT available
- Coming soon to select countries (Q4 2026)

ORDER TRACKING:
- Track ID provided via SMS/Email immediately after order confirmation
- Real-time GPS tracking available on SwiftKart app and website
- SMS updates at: Order placed, Shipped, Out for delivery, Delivered

SHIPPING RESTRICTIONS:
- Hazardous materials (flammables, explosives) cannot be shipped
- Perishable items: Not available for shipping (canned/packaged goods only)
- Oversized items (>25kg): May incur additional charges

RETURNS SHIPPING:
- SwiftKart covers return shipping for defective/damaged items
- Standard return pickup: 3-5 business days
- Customers pay for returns on regular returns (₹99 - ₹199 depending on location)

For shipping inquiries: support@swiftkart.com | Track your order at swiftkart.com/track
