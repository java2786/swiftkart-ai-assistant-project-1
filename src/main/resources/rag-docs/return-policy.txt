SWIFTKART RETURN POLICY

SwiftKart is committed to customer satisfaction. Here is our comprehensive return policy:

RETURN WINDOWS BY PRODUCT CATEGORY:

1. GENERAL MERCHANDISE (Clothing, Home & Kitchen, Books, etc.)
   - Standard Return Window: 30 days from date of delivery
   - Items must be in original, unused condition with all tags attached
   - Refund is issued in full upon inspection and approval

2. ELECTRONICS & TRIMMERS
   - Return Window: STRICTLY 14 days from date of delivery
   - IMPORTANT: Electronics purchased with heavy discounts (>40% off) are NON-RETURNABLE
   - Trimmers and electric shavers must be unopened and unused
   - Returns accepted only if item is defective or damaged during delivery
   - Full refund issued for defective items; discounted items eligible for store credit only

3. PERSONAL CARE & BEAUTY PRODUCTS
   - Opened or partially used items: NON-RETURNABLE (hygiene reasons)
   - Unopened items: 30-day return window
   - Items must be sealed and in original packaging

4. LARGE APPLIANCES
   - Return Window: 14 days from delivery
   - Must be in original packaging and unused
   - Inspection required before approval

RETURN PROCESS:
1. Contact SwiftKart Customer Support within the applicable return window
2. Provide order number and reason for return
3. Arrange return pickup via SwiftExpress or BlueDart (We cover return shipping for defective items)
4. Items received at our warehouse are inspected
5. Refund processed within 5-7 business days after inspection

IMPORTANT EXCEPTIONS:
- Items purchased with OPEN BOX or CLEARANCE tags cannot be returned
- Customized or personalized items are non-returnable
- Accessories bundled as "free gifts" may have different policies (check order details)

DAMAGED IN TRANSIT:
If an item arrives damaged, report within 48 hours. We will arrange replacement or full refund at no cost.
