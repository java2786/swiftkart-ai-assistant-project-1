SWIFTKART FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

PAYMENT & ORDERS

Q: What payment methods does SwiftKart accept?
A: SwiftKart accepts:
   - Credit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
   - Debit Cards (all banks)
   - UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, WhatsApp Pay)
   - Net Banking (all major banks)
   - Wallets (PayTM, Amazon Pay, MobiKwik)
   - Cash on Delivery (COD) - available in selected areas
   
   We DO NOT accept cryptocurrency, checks, or money orders.

Q: Can I use a gift card or voucher?
A: Yes! Gift cards and promotional vouchers can be applied at checkout. The discount will be applied after calculating shipping and taxes.

Q: Is there an exchange option instead of return?
A: Exchange is available for electronics and apparel only (within 14 days of purchase). Contact support@swiftkart.com to initiate an exchange.

REFUNDS & CANCELLATION

Q: How long does a refund take?
A: Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after your return is inspected and approved. The refund appears in your original payment method (may take 2-3 additional business days depending on your bank).

Q: Can I cancel my order?
A: Yes, orders can be canceled within 4 hours of placement. After 4 hours, the order moves to processing and cannot be canceled online. Contact customer support for assistance.

Q: What if my item arrives damaged?
A: Report damage within 48 hours with photos. We'll arrange a replacement or full refund at no cost. No return shipping needed for damaged items.

SHIPPING & DELIVERY

Q: Why hasn't my order been delivered yet?
A: Standard delivery is 3-5 business days. Check the tracking status at swiftkart.com/track using your order number. Delays can occur due to weather, holidays, or remote location.

Q: Can I change my delivery address?
A: Address can be changed only before the order ships (typically within 24 hours of order placement). Use your tracking link or contact support immediately.

Q: Do you deliver on weekends and holidays?
A: Standard delivery operates Monday-Friday. Express and same-day delivery options are available on weekends in selected metro areas.

Q: What if no one is home at delivery?
A: Our delivery partner will attempt delivery twice. You can reschedule via the tracking page or request a specific time slot.

PRODUCT INFORMATION

Q: How do I know if an item is in stock?
A: Check the product page. Green "In Stock" means immediate availability. "Pre-order" items ship within 2-4 weeks. Items not shown are out of stock.

Q: Can I request a product that isn't listed?
A: Yes! Use the "Request a Product" feature on the website or email suggestions@swiftkart.com.

Q: Are there any warranties on electronics?
A: Manufacturer's warranty (1-2 years) applies to all electronics. Extended warranty options available at checkout. Warranty details provided in product description.

CUSTOMER ACCOUNT

Q: How do I create an account?
A: Visit swiftkart.com and click "Sign Up". You can also create an account using your Gmail or Facebook credentials.

Q: Can I track multiple orders at once?
A: Yes, your account dashboard shows all active and past orders with live tracking.

Q: How do I update my profile information?
A: Log in → Account Settings → Edit Profile. You can update address, phone, email, and preferences.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Q: What are your customer support hours?
A: Support is available 24/7 via:
   - Chat: swiftkart.com/support (live chat 9 AM - 9 PM IST, automated chatbot 24/7)
   - Email: support@swiftkart.com (response within 24 hours)
   - Phone: 1-800-SWIFTKART (8 AM - 10 PM IST, Monday-Saturday)

Q: How can I report a defective product?
A: Contact support with:
   1. Order number
   2. Product name
   3. Description of the defect
   4. 2-3 clear photos of the defect
   
   We'll arrange a replacement or refund immediately.

For more help, visit our Help Center at help.swiftkart.com
